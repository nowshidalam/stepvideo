## Key Features

You can edit your tutorial **step by step** instead of working with a traditional video timeline.

## FAQ

### What do I need to record?

Chrome — and nothing else to install. StepVideo records the browser tab you select and works out the steps from what happens in it.

The Chrome extension captures clicks more precisely, helping steps line up with the actions you take and zooms focus on the controls you interact with.

### How are AI minutes counted?

AI minutes are counted against the **rendered video, not the raw recording.**

Dead time is removed before the final duration is counted. For example, a four-minute recording that renders at 1:47 uses about two minutes of your allowance.

### What happens if I make a mistake while recording?

You can **mark a mistake while recording and keep going.** The marked take is removed from the finished video.

With **Perfect Take (beta)**, you can also re-record an individual step without recording the entire workflow again.

### Can I change the voiceover?

Yes. Each step has its own generated script, and you can edit it manually.

When you change the script, the voiceover and captions for that step are regenerated without requiring you to record the entire tutorial again.

### Where does the finished video live?

Each render gets its own **share page**, which can be public or unlisted.

The exported MP4 also includes burned-in captions.

### What happens when the trial ends?

The free trial lasts **10 days** and includes **5 AI video minutes** with no credit card required. Trial exports are watermarked.

When the trial ends, exporting stops until you choose a paid plan. Your existing work is not deleted, and a paid plan allows you to re-export without the watermark and at full quality.
